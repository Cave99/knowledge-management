## Look for an existing answer

Search the knowledge library for your task, the tool you're using, or the words in an error message. The relevant answer might live in a different team's space.

## Ask a useful question

### Explain the goal

Start with what you're trying to do, rather than the workaround you're considering. That gives the other person room to suggest a simpler approach.

### Share the context

- What you expected to happen.
- What actually happened.
- What you've already tried.
- Relevant links or an error message.

### Make the next step clear

Say whether you need a quick pointer, a review, or help investigating. Mention a deadline if there is one.

## Close the loop

When you find the answer, share it in the same conversation. If it could help someone else, ask the document owner to add it to the relevant guide.
