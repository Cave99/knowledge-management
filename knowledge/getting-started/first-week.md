## Find your bearings {#find-your-bearings}

### Meet your team {#meet-your-team}

Ask your team lead who you will work with most often. Learn what each person looks after and the best way to ask for help.

### Set up your tools {#set-up-your-tools}

Check you can access the systems you need. [Finding the right tools and resources](../resources/tools.md) explains how to request anything missing.

## Learn how work happens {#learn-how-work-happens}

### Read the essentials {#read-the-essentials}

Start with the guides your team uses every day. Star the ones you expect to return to and make a note of anything that needs more explanation.

### Follow a process with someone {#follow-a-process-with-someone}

Working through a real example is one of the quickest ways to understand how a guide fits the day-to-day work.

## Ask the small questions {#ask-the-small-questions}

You don't need to work everything out alone. A question that feels obvious often reveals an assumption that deserves to be written down.

Read [Getting help from your team](../team-support/getting-help.md) for a useful way to share context.
