## Your shared source of knowledge {#your-shared-source-of-knowledge}

Good work starts with a little clarity. KM flow brings your team's guides, practical know-how, and everyday processes into one place, so the answer is there when you need it.

This is an example library to help you explore. Every document you see is a real Markdown file. Replace these examples with your team's own knowledge whenever you're ready.

> **A good place to start**
> Browse a space, open a guide, or search for something you need. Star the documents you come back to most.

## Find your way around {#find-your-way-around}

### Explore a space {#explore-a-space}

Spaces bring related knowledge together. **Getting Started** covers the essentials, **Guides & Playbooks** explains how things get done, and **Tools & Resources** points you in the right direction.

### Find an answer {#find-an-answer}

Use the search at the top of any page, or press **⌘K** on Mac or **Ctrl K** on Windows. Search looks through document titles, descriptions, tags, and contents.

### Keep the useful things close {#keep-the-useful-things-close}

Select the star on a document to save it to **Starred**. Your **Recently viewed** list remembers where you've been on this device.

## Make knowledge a team habit {#make-knowledge-a-team-habit}

| Habit | Who | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Read the team essentials | Everyone | First week |
| Check a process still works | Document owner | Each quarter |
| Capture a useful answer | Whoever finds it | As it happens |
| Share a change with the team | Process owner | When the process changes |
